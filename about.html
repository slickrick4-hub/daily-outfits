About this site

Outfits on Sale helps people put together complete outfits from clothing and shoes that are on sale right now.

How it works

Every morning the site checks the public product lists of a set of clothing and shoe brands and keeps the items that are marked down and in stock. Each visitor then enters their own sizes, budget and taste, and the site assembles outfits from those items: a top, pants and shoes, sometimes with a layer. Outfits are matched by color, formality and pattern so the pieces go together, not just by price.

Visitors can take a short style quiz, love or swap individual pieces, and save looks they like. The site learns from those choices, but only in that visitor’s own browser.

Where the products come from

Product names, photos, prices and sizes come from the retailers’ own public websites. The site is not owned by, sponsored by or officially connected to any of the brands shown. Brand names and images belong to their owners, and every product links back to the retailer’s own page, where you complete any purchase.

Affiliate disclosure

Right now, the links on this site are plain links to the retailers. If I join affiliate programs, some links will become affiliate links, which means I may earn a small commission if you buy something, at no extra cost to you. This page will be updated if that happens, and affiliate links will be labeled. Outfits are chosen by your sizes, budget and taste, not by which retailer pays a commission.

Prices and availability

Prices and sizes change quickly, and the site’s information may be out of date by the time you look. Prices shown in Canadian dollars are converted from the store’s own currency at an approximate rate, and may not include shipping, duties or taxes. A “sale” price is whatever the retailer lists as a discount, and I can’t verify that it is a true markdown. Always check the retailer’s page before you buy. Items marked “final sale” usually can’t be returned.

Privacy

There are no accounts. Your sizes, budget, style choices, saved looks and loved pieces are stored only in your own browser and are never sent to me. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and product photos from the retailers’ websites, so those services can see that your browser requested them. I don’t use advertising or tracking tools on this site.
